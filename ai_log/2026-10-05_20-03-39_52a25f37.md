## AI Summary

Usage credits don't buy a separate pool for cloud sessions. They let you keep working after you hit your plan's usage limit, and they cover the same usage everywhere.

- **What they are:** extra usage on top of your Pro, Max or Team subscription. Once you reach your plan's limit, credits let you continue instead of waiting for the limit to reset.
- **Managing them:** you run `/usage-credits`, which opens the page you just saw at claude.ai/settings/usage. It only works when you're signed in with your claude.ai subscription through `/login`. It isn't available if you authenticate with an API key.
- **Cloud sessions use the same limits:** a cloud session is Claude Code running on Anthropic's infrastructure instead of your laptop. It counts against the same limits as the rest of your Claude and Claude Code usage. The cloud machine itself has no extra charge.
